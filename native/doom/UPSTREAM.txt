This engine derives from id Software Doom and Chocolate Doom. Copyright notices
remain in every source file. The engine and tapok_sdl.c are distributed under
GPL-2.0-or-later; see COPYING and the individual source headers. The repository's
TAPOK-256 license does not apply to this directory. The executable is a separate
process, not linked into the Rust application.

No IWAD, maps, textures, music, or other game data are included. Supply a legally
obtained compatible Doom IWAD or freely licensed Freedoom IWAD separately.

Local integration files: Makefile, tapok_sdl.c. Network multiplayer is not
implemented by DoomGeneric. SDL2_mixer is optional; without it sound and music
are disabled. Music with SDL2_mixer also requires an available MIDI synthesizer
and its instrument data (for example a configured soundfont).
